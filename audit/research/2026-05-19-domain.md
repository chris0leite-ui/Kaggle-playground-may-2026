# Domain research — 2026-05-19 (real-driver gap)

Triggered by: today's plateau finding. Model is 120 bp worse on
real-F1 driver subset (3-letter codes; 38% of rows, 46% of positives,
AUC 0.947) vs synthetic D### subset (AUC 0.959). Synthetic data
preserved track/compound/lap structure but stripped per-driver and
per-team behaviour. Goal: enumerate ACTIONABLE external features
joinable on real drivers, with explicit imputation for the 62%
synthetic remainder.

## Top decision drivers of a real F1 pit (literature)

1. **Tyre-compound deg curve × circuit**. Pirelli compounds C1-C5 are
   re-mapped to HARD/MEDIUM/SOFT per event; per-track nomination
   shifts the deg curve by a full step. e.g. Bahrain 2024 used C1/C2/C3
   (hardest range), Australia 2024 C3/C4/C5 (softest). Pit window is
   roughly when the live-pace deg crosses the undercut benefit (~1.5-
   2.0 s on outlap). (S1, S2, S6)
2. **Circuit pit-loss & safety-car prior**. Singapore: SC probability
   ~100%, clustered around laps 1 and ~30 of 62; medium-start window
   laps 21-27, soft-start 14-19, hard-start 37-43. Monaco: low SC
   probability but narrow overtake window; 2025 forces a 2-stop.
   Spa: 44 laps, narrow strategic window driven by long lap. (S3, S4)
3. **VSC / SC time discount**. Green-flag pit ~20-25 s lost; under
   VSC/SC the relative loss drops to ~10-12 s. Teams pit
   opportunistically; this is the single largest non-tyre lever and
   the dataset's synthetic generator likely strips it. Possible
   residual signature: position_change spike + cluster of pits in
   same lap window. (S4, S5)
4. **Team pit-execution speed** (median pit time, 2025: Ferrari
   2.35 s, Mercedes 2.50 s, Racing Bulls 2.67 s, Sauber 2.7+, Red
   Bull 3.10 s). Faster crews enable earlier offensive undercuts
   without losing track position; weak crews bias toward fewer /
   later stops. (S7, S8)
5. **Driver tyre management** style. Aggressive (push-pull) drivers
   pit earlier on softs; conservative tyre-managers extend stints
   1-stop. Stable across seasons for veterans (HAM, VER, ALO).
   Captured by per-driver historical mean stint length conditional
   on compound + circuit. (S9, S10)
6. **2-different-compound rule** (FIA): dry races force ≥1 pit;
   Monaco 2025 mandates ≥2. The raw "must-pit-by-lap-X" deadline
   is a hard structural feature on RaceProgress.

## Three rank-ordered features (predicted lift)

### F1. PerCircuit-Compound pit-lap quantile (highest)
For each (Race, Compound), join historical median + IQR pit-in lap
number from Ergast/FastF1 2018-2024 (filter dry races only).
Encode `lap_vs_median = LapNumber - median_pit_lap[(Race,Compound)]`
and `lap_in_q1q3 = LapNumber ∈ [q1, q3]` indicator. Lift expected:
real-driver subset closes most of the 120 bp gap because pit-window
is what the synthetic generator most clearly preserved at population
level but not at driver level. Join risk: applies equally to ALL
rows (no driver join needed). **Lowest-risk add.**

### F2. Per-Driver historical pit-aggressiveness (next)
For each Driver (3-letter), compute mean stint length residual:
`avg(stint_length - circuit_median_stint_length)` over 2018-2024
Ergast/FastF1 data. Adds a continuous "aggressive (negative) ↔
conservative (positive)" axis. Join key: Driver code. Risk: synthetic
D### codes have no match — impute with **global mean (zero residual
by construction) AND a `driver_is_synthetic` flag column** so the
model can learn the imputation regime separately. Without the flag
this would leak distribution mismatch.

### F3. Per-Team pit-execution time + per-(Driver,Race,Year) Ergast
historical pit count (tied 3rd)
Map Driver → Team (drivers.csv → constructorId via results.csv per
year) → constructor median pit-stop time from f1pace.com / Ergast
duration field. Faster crew → earlier undercut probability. Join
key: (Driver, Year). Risk: D### synthetic drivers have no constructor
— impute median team time (~2.6 s) plus same `team_is_synthetic`
flag. Bonus: a direct (Driver, Race, Year)→pit-laps lookup gives a
near-label feature for the real subset; must be fold-safe (build
from training-fold rows only per R24) but the external Kaggle
dataset is fixed historical data so leakage is bounded to whatever
race-year combos overlap test.

## Risk register

- **Test-set Year coverage**: confirm test races are within 2018-
  2024 before committing FastF1 effort; if test contains 2025+ races
  Ergast is dead and we need FastF1 only.
- **Race-name normalisation**: "British GP" vs "Silverstone" vs
  "Great Britain". Build an alias table.
- **2-compound rule** means a "lap-of-mandatory-pit" hard constraint
  by RaceProgress; encode as `laps_remaining_if_no_pit_yet`.
- **Synthetic-flag interaction**: every external feature MUST ship
  with a `_is_imputed` companion column; otherwise tree splits will
  conflate synth-imputed real drivers with genuine outliers.
