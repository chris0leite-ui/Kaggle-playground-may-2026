## Mechanism finding

**Path-B's LR-meta absorbs cohort-listwise as one direction**. Multiple
listwise bases added to K=N+1 pool only contribute a single linear
coefficient → diminishing returns. R20 (LGB lambdarank) and R21 (CB
YetiRank) both showed orthogonal standalone OOF (ρ 0.75-0.98 vs R17)
but at K=19 add the LR-meta collapsed them onto the same direction.

**Submission-level rank-blend bypasses this**. Combining R21's
PRE-LR-meta standalone OOF (high AUC, orthogonal direction) with K=18's
POST-LR-meta output extracts the orthogonal signal that the LR-meta
couldn't. This is the mechanism that produced R25's +0.05 bp LB lift.
